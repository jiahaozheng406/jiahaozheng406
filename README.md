**Undergraduate Researcher** at **Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT)**  
📍 Majoring in **Internet of Things Engineering**

### 🚀 Research Philosophy

I am an AI researcher trying to connect **3D visual understanding**, **physical-world signals**, and **interactive generation**. My work currently focuses on:

- 🧊 **3D Reconstruction & 3DGS** - Building stable 3D representations from sparse and imperfect observations
- ⚛️ **AI for Quantum Sensing** - Learning from noisy physical signals with physics-guided modeling
- 🤖 **Robot Vision with Hardware Systems** - Connecting perception, deployment, embedded hardware, and action
- 📐 **CAD / 3D Content Generation** - Exploring structured 3D assets, editable geometry, and design-oriented generation
- 🌐 **World-model-oriented Thinking** - Moving from "seeing the scene" toward predicting, editing, and interacting with it

### 🎯 Fun Fact: Call me **"++"**!

> 💡 It's a clever pun on my Chinese name:
> - 🔤 **Jiahao** sounds like "Plus Sign" (加号)
> - ➕ **Zheng** means "Positive" (正)
> 
> So mathematically, I am literally **`++`** 😄  
> Like the increment operator - always improving, always growing!

### 🏃‍♂️ The Runner's Mindset

<img align="right" alt="Running GIF" width="380" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/74038190/229223156-143a1f73-bbd9-4287-bdb6-362948549a5c.gif">

I am an avid **marathon runner**, believing that the endurance required on the track mirrors the persistence needed in scientific research.

> **"Every kilometer on the track is a metaphor for every epoch in training neural networks."**

**What Running Teaches Me:**
- **Patience** - Models converge slowly, just like endurance builds gradually
- **Resilience** - Hitting the wall at km 30 ≈ NaN loss at epoch 87
- **Optimization** - Finding the right pace ≈ Finding the right learning rate
